# India Job Market Analytics 📊

## Project Overview
India Job Market Analytics is a data analytics project that explores job opportunities in India, focusing on analytics-related roles. The project uses Python for data preparation and Power BI to build interactive dashboards that help analyze job openings, salary ranges, locations, experience requirements, and company hiring trends.

## Objectives
- Analyze analytics-related job opportunities in India.
- Identify locations with the most job openings.
- Explore experience requirements across job postings.
- Compare salary ranges across companies.
- Identify companies with the highest number of job openings.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Python** — Data filtering and preparation
- **Pandas** — Data manipulation
- **Power BI** — Interactive dashboards and visualizations
- **CSV / Excel** — Dataset storage and processing

## Dataset
The project uses an India job market dataset containing job titles, company names, locations, experience requirements, salary information, and skills.

The dashboard analyzes a filtered dataset containing **906 job postings**.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. India Job Market Analytics
- Total job postings
- Average minimum and maximum salary
- Job openings by location
- Experience distribution
- Top companies
- Interactive location and job-title filters

### 2. Company Hiring & Salary Analysis
- Total job postings
- Average salary range by company
- Companies ranked by job openings
